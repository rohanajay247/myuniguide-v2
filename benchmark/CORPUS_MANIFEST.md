# CORPUS_MANIFEST

Synthetic German university regulation corpus for MyUniGuide V2.
Generated 2026-09-02. Corpus as-of date **2025-10-01** (WS 2025/26).

**19 documents · 296 pages · 2 universities · 4 programmes · 108 modules · 72 benchmark questions**

Every document is generated deterministically from the machine-readable source layer in
`corpus_build/data/`. No document was hand-written as a PDF.

## 4. Version families

```
VELMORA / ASPO
  V1 (1.0, 2019-09-01)
     ├── V2  Ä1  2021-09-01   cohort-based:  cohort_start >= WS 2021/22
     └── V3  Ä2  2024-03-01   date-based + grandfathering for theses registered earlier
  V4  Lesefassung = 1.0 + Ä1 + Ä2   (non-binding)

RHEINMARK / APO
  R1 (1.0, 2021-03-01)
     └── R2  Ä1  2023-10-01   cohort-based OR irrevocable election by 2024-09-30
  R3  Konsolidierte Fassung = 1.0 + Ä1   (non-binding)
```

Amendments are evaluated **independently**, never as a chain. A Velmora student who missed
Ä1 by cohort still receives Ä2 by date. This produces four distinct effective ASPO states:

| State | Ä1 | Ä2 | Applies to | Equals V4? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | ✗ | ✓ | cohorts ≤ SS 2021, no open pre-2024 thesis | no |
| B | ✓ | ✓ | cohorts ≥ WS 2021/22, no open pre-2024 thesis | **yes** |
| A0 | ✗ | ✗ | cohorts ≤ SS 2021 with open pre-2024 thesis | no |
| B0 | ✓ | ✗ | cohorts ≥ WS 2021/22 with open pre-2024 thesis | no |

States A and A0 contain the same number of sections and paragraphs but different text.
Structural counting alone cannot distinguish them.

APO has two states: **P** (no amendment) and **Q** (Ä1 applied); R3 equals Q only.

## 7. Intentional findings

**Four genuine conflicts** — comparable authority, simultaneously in force, no precedence rule:

| ID | Where | Collision | Gating |
|---|---|---|---|
| K-01 | HRM / IB | FSB § 14 Abs. 2 (120 LP) vs its own binding Annex 1 footnote 4 (150 LP) | always |
| K-02 | HVM / ET | ASPO § 31 Abs. 1 as amended (4 months, "Abweichungen sind nicht zulässig") vs BSPO-ET § 12 Abs. 2 (3 months) | states B, B0 only |
| K-03 | HRM / SE | SPP-SE Anlage 1 (Kl-120) vs MHB-SE Prüfungsform field (PA), both binding | always |
| K-04 | HVM / IAI | ASPO § 33 Abs. 3 Satz 2 (prohibitive, from Ä2) vs BSPO-IAI § 13 Abs. 4 (mandatory) | state B only |

Everything else that looks like a contradiction is classified separately and must **not**
be reported as a conflict: 8 legitimate overrides (each with an express permission trail),
3 authority cases, 4 version-dependent reference cases, 9 near-duplicate provisions,
8 absence cases. See `ground_truth/intentional_conflicts.yaml`.

Two traps worth naming:
- **O-05** — FSB-SE deviates on thesis processing time and the deviation is *missing* from
  the Anlage 3 overview. § 8 Abs. 4 APO makes that overview declaratory, so the deviation
  is still valid. A system treating the overview as constitutive answers wrongly.
- **A-01 vs A-04** — "Is a Hausarbeit permissible?" is **no** at Velmora (exhaustive
  catalogue since Ä2) and **yes** at Rheinmark (open catalogue). Generalising the Velmora
  rule is punished.
